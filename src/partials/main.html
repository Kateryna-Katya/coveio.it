Neon Spirit Academy

About the Game

A story-driven anime RPG built around spirit powers
At Neon Spirit Academy, you’re one of the chosen few who can tap into these ancient spirit powers — and trust me, once you do, you’re thrown headfirst into some seriously supernatural battles. You’ll be putting together your dream team, leveling them up, diving into their stories, and taking on some seriously tough bosses. It’s not just about getting better stats — these characters actually develop as people, and honestly, that’s what makes the fights feel way more important, which makes you care way more about how they perform in battle. We want it to feel like you’re actually in an anime — all those dramatic moments and emotional beats that make you actually care about what happens.
